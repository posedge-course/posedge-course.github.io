Syllabus

Thirteen levels, each a few short lessons, a project and a checkpoint; the last two take apart two real designs. Pass a level's checkpoint to open the next. You can take any checkpoint to skip ahead, and every lesson stays readable whatever level you are on.

LevelThemeWhat you learnState
0What an FPGA isCheckpointWhat is inside an FPGA, and how code becomes hardware.Open
1Combinational logicCheckpointSignals, operators and multiplexers, and the latch you did not mean to build.Locked
2Clocks and flip-flopsCheckpointHow a design remembers things, and why everything waits for the clock.Locked
3SimulationCheckpointTestbenches that check themselves, and waveforms you can read.Locked
4Building blocksCheckpointCounters, shift registers, edge detectors and a debounced button.Locked
5State machinesCheckpointStates, transitions and outputs, up to a working UART transmitter.Locked
6From code to hardwareCheckpointWhat synthesis builds from your code, and how to read its report.Locked
7Constraints and the boardCheckpointPins, the clock constraint and programming, and what to check when the board does nothing.Locked
8TimingCheckpointSetup, hold, slack and the critical path, and how to fix a failing one.Locked
9Crossing clock domainsCheckpointMetastability, synchronisers, handshakes and asynchronous FIFOs.Locked
10InterfacesCheckpointValid and ready, FIFOs, a UART receiver and debugging on the board.Locked
11Case study: 2D neural decoderCheckpointA decoder for quantum error correction on the course's board, taken apart block by block.Locked
12Case study: 3D neural decoderCheckpointA decoder that reads several rounds of checks at once, taken apart on the course's board.Locked
